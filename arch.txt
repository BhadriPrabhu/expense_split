Key Clean Architecture Rules for this App
Keep Math in Domain (UseCases):

Put your Split Math (dividing totals by percentages/shares) and Debt Simplification Algorithm inside domain/usecases/ pure Dart files. Never put math inside BLoCs or UI Widgets!

Models vs. Entities:

ExpenseEntity lives in Domain. It contains core properties like id, amount, paidByUserId, splits.

ExpenseModel lives in Data. It extends ExpenseEntity and adds fromJson() and toJson() methods for database/API mapping.

Keep State Independent:

expenses feature depends on auth (to know who created the expense) and groups (to attach the expense to a group), but it interacts with them through IDs (groupId, userId), keeping the modules clean.
